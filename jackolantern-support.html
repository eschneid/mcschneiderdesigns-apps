Jack-o'-Lantern Studio lets you carve a pumpkin with your finger, drop a candle inside, and watch it glow. Carve your own design freehand or trace a stencil, then share your jack-o'-lantern or save it to your photos. No mess, no seeds.

How to Carve

  • Put your finger on the pumpkin and drag. The knife follows your finger, and it cuts where its tip is, a little above your fingertip, so you can see what you're carving.
  • Cut a closed shape and that piece comes out of the pumpkin
  • Pinch with two fingers to zoom in for fine details, and drag with two fingers to move around
  • Tap Light to drop a candle inside, and Lights out to darken the room and see it glow
  • Undo takes back the last cut. New starts a fresh pumpkin.

Stencils

  • Tap a stencil in the tray to show its dashed guide lines on the pumpkin
  • Trace the lines with the knife, or tap Carve it to cut the whole stencil at once
  • Wipe lines hides the guide so you can see your carving clean
  • Classic, Happy and Spooky are free. The Creeps & Critters pack adds Cat, Vampire, Bat, Skull and Haunted as a one-time purchase.

Pumpkin Shapes

Choose a Round, Tall or Fat pumpkin with the arrows under it, or swipe across the screen starting off the pumpkin. Stencils fit themselves to each shape. Switching shapes starts a fresh pumpkin, so the app checks with you first if you've already carved.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I keep my carving?

Tap Share to send a picture of it, or on iPhone and iPad tap Save to put it in your Photos. Carvings aren't kept when you close the app, so save the ones you like.

How do I save to my photos on Android?

Tap Share, then choose your gallery, Google Photos, or Files from the share sheet.

I said no to Photos access. How do I turn it back on?

Open your device's Settings, find Jack-o'-Lantern Studio, tap Photos, and allow it to add photos. Share works without it.

I bought Creeps & Critters. How do I get it on a new device?

Sign in with the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with, then tap Restore purchases under the stencil tray. The pack unlocks at no extra charge. If your payment was still pending, the pack unlocks as soon as it goes through.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. Carving, lighting, sharing and saving all work offline. Buying or restoring a stencil pack needs a connection to the App Store or Google Play.

Does the app collect my data?

No. There are no ads, no analytics and no accounts. See the Privacy Policy for details.

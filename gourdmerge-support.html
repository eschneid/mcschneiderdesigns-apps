Gourd Merge is a fall physics puzzle game. Drop gourds into the harvest crate, match two of a kind to grow them into the next gourd on the vine, and try to raise a Blue-Ribbon Giant before the crate spills over.

How to Play

Drag across the crate to aim, then let go to drop the gourd. When two identical gourds touch, they merge into the next, bigger gourd and you score points. The game ends when the crate stays filled past the dashed line at the top for a couple of seconds.

Frequently Asked Questions

What order do the gourds grow in?

  • Pumpkin Seed
  • Wee Gourd
  • Warty Gourd
  • Pattypan
  • Delicata
  • Acorn Squash
  • Butternut
  • Kabocha
  • Cinderella
  • Jack-o'-Lantern
  • Blue-Ribbon Giant

The vine along the bottom of the screen shows the full order and how far you have gotten in the current game.

How does scoring work?

Each merge scores more the bigger the gourd it makes. Merging two Blue-Ribbon Giants clears them both for a 100-point bonus.

How do I start over?

Tap the circular arrow button at the top right, then tap it again to confirm.

How do I turn the sound off?

Tap the speaker button at the top right. The app remembers your choice.

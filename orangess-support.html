Orangess is an ambient visualizer that fills your screen with flowing, glowing orange light: lava, tunnels, kaleidoscopes, sunbursts and spirals that drift and blend into one another. Put it on for mood lighting, a party, or just to relax. It works with or without music.

How to Use

  • Drag your finger across the screen to make ripples, like moving your hand through water
  • Tap to show the controls. They fade away on their own after a few seconds.
  • The scene changes by itself every half minute or so, and the shade of orange slowly shifts over time

The Controls

  • Next (the arrow): skip to a new scene
  • Microphone: turn music mode on or off
  • − and +: slow the visuals down or speed them up

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I make it move to music?

Tap the screen, then tap the microphone button. The first time, your device asks for microphone permission. Play music nearby and the visuals will sway with the bass. Tap the microphone again to turn it off.

Does the app record me?

No. In music mode the app only measures how loud the sound is, live on your device. Nothing is recorded, saved, or sent anywhere, and the microphone turns off when you leave the app. See the Privacy Policy for details.

I said no to the microphone. How do I turn it back on?

Open your device's Settings, find Orangess, and allow the microphone. Everything except music mode works without it.

Will it keep my screen on?

Yes. While Orangess is open, your screen won't dim or lock, so you can leave it running. Keep your device plugged in for long sessions.

Does it flash or strobe?

No. Orangess is designed to move smoothly, with no flashing effects. Even in music mode, the visuals ease with the beat rather than flash. If you are sensitive to motion or light, please use your own judgment.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. Orangess works completely offline.

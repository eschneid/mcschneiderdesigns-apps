Orangess — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026

Orangess ("the app," "we," "us") is an ambient visualizer that fills your screen with moving orange light, built by McSchneider Design Studios. This policy explains what information the app uses and how. The short version: the app collects nothing about you, and everything it uses stays on your device. The only exception is buying Orangess Pro, which goes through Google Play or the Apple App Store (see section 5).

1. Information We Collect

No account required. Orangess does not require you to create an account or provide any personal information to use the app.

We collect no personal data. The app does not collect, store on our servers, or transmit your name, email, location, contacts, photos, identifiers, usage statistics, or any other personal information. We have no servers for the app at all. It contains no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking.

2. Microphone

Orangess has an optional music mode that lets the visuals move with sound around you. It is off by default and only turns on when you tap the microphone button. Your device will ask for microphone permission the first time.

While music mode is on:

  • Sound is analyzed live, on your device, only to measure how loud the music is and how strong the bass is at that moment
  • Audio is never recorded, saved, or sent anywhere, and no one else can hear it
  • The microphone is released as soon as you turn music mode off or leave the app

You can decline the permission and still use every other part of the app. You can also revoke microphone access at any time in your device settings.

Motion: shaking your phone swirls the picture. The app reads the motion sensor on your device only to notice a shake; nothing is recorded or stored.

3. Your Photos and Saved Pictures

Your photos as scenes (Orangess Pro, optional): you can pick up to three photos to turn into glowing scenes. You choose each one in your device's own photo picker, so the app only ever receives the photos you pick, never your library. A smaller copy of each chosen photo is kept inside the app on your device and is never uploaded or shared. You can remove them in the app at any time. On iPhone and iPad, the picker can also offer to take a new photo, which is why the app may ask for camera access; the camera is only used if you choose that option.

Saving pictures and Live Photos (Orangess Pro, optional): when you tap "Save picture" or "Live Photo", the app adds that image to your photo library so you can use it as a wallpaper. On iPhone and iPad this uses add-only access: the app can add pictures but cannot see your library.

4. Notifications (optional)

If you turn on the bedtime reminder, the app schedules a daily reminder at the time you choose, plus an occasional seasonal note (for example, when the Halloween scenes return). These notifications are scheduled on your device; there is no push server and nothing is sent to us. Reminders are off unless you turn them on, and you can turn them off in the app or in your device settings at any time.

5. Purchases (Orangess Pro)

Orangess Pro is a one-time in-app purchase made through Google Play or the Apple App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Google or Apple; we never see or receive your payment details. The app only asks the store whether your store account owns Orangess Pro, so it can unlock Pro features and restore them on a new device. Google and Apple provide developers with standard sales records (such as an order number, date, country and price), which we use only for accounting and to help with support requests. Their handling of your purchase is covered by Google's Privacy Policy and Apple's Privacy Policy.

Now and then the app may ask, through the store's own rating window, whether you'd like to rate Orangess. Any rating or review you leave goes to the store, not to us.

6. Information Stored on Your Device

The app keeps your settings on your device so they're there next time: things like your sound, scene and studio choices, favorite moments (including small snapshots of the screen), your playlist, alarm and reminder times, the photos you chose, whether you've seen the first-launch tip, and whether Pro is unlocked. None of this leaves your device.

7. Third-Party Services

Orangess has no analytics, advertising or tracking services and no servers of its own. It only talks to the app store you installed it from, using the store's own purchase and rating features (Google Play Billing and In-App Review on Android, StoreKit on iPhone and iPad). Everything else works entirely offline.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Because we collect no personal data, there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Everything the app keeps is on your device: you can remove it by clearing the app's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the app. Your Orangess Pro purchase stays with your Google or Apple account, so it can be restored after reinstalling.

9. Children's Privacy

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Purchases are subject to the store's own parental controls.

10. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, for example if a future version adds a feature that uses data differently, we will update this page and the effective date above before that version is released.

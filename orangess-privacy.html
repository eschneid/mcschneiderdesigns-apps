Orangess — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 27, 2026

Orangess ("the app," "we," "us") is an ambient visualizer that fills your screen with moving orange light, built by McSchneider Design Studios. This policy explains what information the app uses and how. The short version: the app collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere.

1. Information We Collect

No account required. Orangess does not require you to create an account or provide any personal information to use the app.

We collect no personal data. The app does not collect, store, or transmit your name, email, location, contacts, photos, identifiers, usage statistics, or any other personal information. It contains no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking.

2. Microphone

Orangess has an optional music mode that lets the visuals move with sound around you. It is off by default and only turns on when you tap the microphone button. Your device will ask for microphone permission the first time.

While music mode is on:

  • Sound is analyzed live, on your device, only to measure how loud the music is and how strong the bass is at that moment
  • Audio is never recorded, saved, or sent anywhere, and no one else can hear it
  • The microphone is released as soon as you turn music mode off or leave the app

You can decline the permission and still use every other part of the app. You can also revoke microphone access at any time in your device settings.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

The app saves one small setting on your device: whether you've already seen the first-launch tip, so it isn't shown again. This never leaves your device.

4. Third-Party Services

Orangess does not use any third-party services, SDKs, or servers. The app works entirely offline.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Because we collect no data, there is nothing for us to retain or delete. You can remove the one on-device setting by clearing the app's storage in your device settings or uninstalling the app.

6. Children's Privacy

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, for example if a future version adds a feature that uses data differently, we will update this page and the effective date above before that version is released.

Gourd Merge — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 24, 2026  ·  Last updated: September 25, 2026

Gourd Merge ("the app," "we," "us") is a physics puzzle game built by McSchneider Design Studios. This policy explains what information the app handles and your choices about it.

1. Information We Collect

No account required. Gourd Merge does not require you to create an account or provide any personal information to play.

Data stored on your device. The app saves your best score, your sound on/off setting, and a count of games played (used to space out ads) in local storage on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to any server.

Advertising data. The app shows occasional full-screen ads between games, served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect certain device information and identifiers, such as your device's advertising ID, to serve and measure ads. Please see Google's privacy policy for details on how AdMob handles data.

What we do not collect. We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone data, or any other personally identifiable information, and we do not run analytics.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Your best score and settings are stored locally so they are there the next time you play
  • Advertising data is used by Google AdMob to serve ads between games

We do not sell or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Third-Party Services

The app relies on the following third-party service. It has its own privacy policy.

  • Google AdMob (advertising) — policies.google.com/privacy

4. Advertising and Opt-Out

You may opt out of personalized advertising at any time through your device settings under Google › Ads › Opt out of Ads Personalization on Android, or Settings › Privacy & Security › Apple Advertising on iOS.

Consent for users in the EEA and UK. If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or another region where consent is legally required, the app presents a consent request — managed through Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) — before any ads are requested. You may accept or decline; if you decline, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalized. You can change your choice at any time with the Ad privacy settings link at the bottom of the game screen.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Your best score and settings stay on your device until you remove them. You can delete them at any time by clearing the app's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the app.

6. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, please contact us and we will address it promptly.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the effective date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

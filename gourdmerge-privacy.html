Gourd Merge — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 24, 2026

Gourd Merge ("the app," "we," "us") is a physics puzzle game built by McSchneider Design Studios. This policy explains what information the app handles and your choices about it.

1. Information We Collect

None. Gourd Merge does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, and no analytics.

Data stored on your device. The app saves your best score and your sound on/off setting in local storage on your device so they are there the next time you play. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to any server.

What we do not collect. We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone data, device identifiers, or any other personally identifiable information.

2. Third-Party Services

The app does not use any third-party services that collect data. It works fully offline.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

Your best score and sound setting stay on your device until you remove them. You can delete them at any time by clearing the app's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the app.

4. Children's Privacy

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

5. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the effective date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

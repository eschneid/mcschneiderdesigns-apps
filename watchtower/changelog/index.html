What's Changed

Watchtower

Every release, newest first. Back to Watchtower

2.11.02026-10-20

  • The Library tab splits into three views — Upcoming, Up Next and All Shows, just under the filters. Every show is still there under All Shows.
  • Search as you type. Tap + and results appear after three letters. The Search button stays for short titles like 24 or ER.
  • Up Next shows more now that it has a screen of its own, and a series you have just added stays at the top instead of sinking to the bottom.
  • Premieres are separated from returning shows. A brand-new series is no longer filed under "Coming Back", which it had never been part of.
  • A season that starts today reads as airing rather than upcoming.
  • The version at the bottom of Profile now shows the build number too.

2.10.02026-10-11

  • See which friends are watching the same show. Open a show and, if any of your friends have it, a count appears next to the rating — tap it to see who is watching, who finished and who gave up.
  • Add a show straight from a friend's card on the Friends tab, without opening it first.
  • Friend requests send a notification, both when somebody asks and when they accept.
  • The Friends tab was reorganised so your own activity stops taking permanent space.
  • Tap an actor's photo to see it full size.

2.9.02026-10-09

  • Mark seasons and episodes watched out of order. Open any show and find Seasons, under My Progress. Tick a whole season, or open one and tick single episodes. Watching series five before series one now says exactly that, and Up Next offers the earliest thing you have not seen.

2.8September 2026

  • Watchtower on your home screen. An Up Next widget and a Tonight widget, in three sizes, with artwork.
  • Tick an episode off without opening the app, straight from the widget.
  • Rows show how far through a show you are.

2.7September 2026

  • Up Next, at the top of your library: pick up exactly where you left off, with the whole queue one tap away.
  • Correct an air date when a show reaches your country later than TMDb says.
  • See what is left on a list.

2.6September 2026

  • Make your own lists, ranked or unranked.
  • Bring lists with you from IMDb, or from a web page.

2.5August 2026

  • Bring your library with you — import from TV Time, Trakt or IMDb, and export your own.
  • Explore a cast member and everything they have been in.
  • See the other films in a series.
